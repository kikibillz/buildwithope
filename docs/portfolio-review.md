The portfolio now leads with three working HR/data projects: workforce reporting, recruitment/onboarding operations and CSV cleanup. Each has an interactive tool, source link, case study and real local browser screenshot. Four tool views are available in the HR reporting desk.

The earlier unsupported savings, performance and production-reliability claims remain removed. Employment history is separate from self-directed portfolio work. The local FAQ is deterministic and makes no model or company-policy retrieval claim. Source and deployment links remain distinct.
